About

I’m a computer science and engineering student who enjoys understanding how things work, then explaining them simply. That habit comes from tutoring: teaching middle and high school students has taught me to break hard ideas into small, clear steps.

Alongside my studies I work as a graphic designer, making logos, posters and social media visuals. Code and design feed each other: one gives me structure, the other gives me taste.

I’m open to internships, freelance design work, and collaborations on student or open-source projects.
